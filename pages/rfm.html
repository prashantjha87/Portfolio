RFM Customer Segmentation

Leveraging Recency, Frequency, and Monetary analytics in SAS Viya to categorize customers into 64 distinct clusters and drive precision marketing.

Customer Segmentation SAS Viya Predictive Analytics Marketing Strategy

The Challenge

In today's highly competitive retail and e-commerce landscape, businesses frequently struggle to identify their highest-value customer segments and target marketing campaigns effectively. Our client faced declining campaign ROIs and lacked granular visibility into customer buying habits, leading to blanket promotional spending with minimal conversion.

Figure 1: RFM score distribution across customer segments.

Solution & Methodology

We developed an advanced RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation engine using analytics platforms to score and categorize the entire customer database:

  • Recency (R): Evaluated how recently a customer interacted with the brand through purchases or site visits.
  • Frequency (F): Quantified purchase frequency to measure customer loyalty and engagement cadence.
  • Monetary Value (M): Measured total spend to identify high-net-worth VIP buyers.

By dividing each of the three dimensions into four equal distribution quartiles, we created 64 distinct customer clusters (4x4x4 matrix). This allowed the client to tailor bespoke marketing retention strategies for champions, loyalists, at-risk churners, and dormant accounts.

Business Impact & Key Outcomes

  • Optimized Marketing Spend: Reallocated promotional budget away from low-yield broad campaigns toward hyper-targeted segment offers.
  • Reduced Churn Rate: Successfully re-engaged at-risk high-monetary customers through automated win-back retention sequences.
  • Increased Customer Lifetime Value (LTV): Improved cross-sell conversion rates by delivering personalized product recommendations aligned with segment purchasing behavior.
